Thanks for testing Nous Deux! It takes two: send the same TestFlight link to your partner, then pair your accounts with a code or a QR code.

Worth trying: the shared calendar, the cycle, location (only when you turn it on), lists, little notes and their notifications. The app is in French or English, following your iPhone's language.

Something wrong? Take a screenshot in the app: TestFlight offers to send it with a comment.
